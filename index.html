The Alchemist’s Handbook

Results not guaranteed.

  1. Brew. Press Brew Potion. Every potion uses one liter of water.
  2. Sell. Customers buy on their own. A lower price sells faster, a higher price earns more per bottle. Try to sell about as fast as you brew.
  3. Keep the barrels full. Buy water before it runs out. The price drifts, and rain makes it cheaper.
  4. Grow. Hire apprentices to brew for you, and advertise to draw a bigger crowd.
  5. Mana and the to-do list. In time you gain mana. Spend it on the to-do list to change the business.
  6. Read the paper. It knows more than it lets on.
